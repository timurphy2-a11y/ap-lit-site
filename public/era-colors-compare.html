Issue 5 — Era Colors

src/components/APLitTimeline.jsx · ERAS[].color

The 5 timeline eras don't map to the 6-unit Separated palette — they cover 1590–1955 literary history. Current colors are dark-mode (designed for a near-black surface); some read fine on cream, others are too muddy or low-contrast. Proposed values keep the same hue family per era but adjust for readability and vibrancy on paper.
